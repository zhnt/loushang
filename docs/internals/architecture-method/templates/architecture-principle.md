## Statement And Rationale

State the condition, preference or review heuristic precisely, where it applies,
why it matters, and its important consequences. Identify relevant limits and
trade-offs. Do not present a feature request or an implementation recipe as a
durable principle.

## Verification

State what a reviewer or executable check observes, which evidence supports the
claim, and what would violate the principle. Distinguish an actual violation
from a heuristic that only calls for further investigation.

## Conflicts And Exceptions

State the authority that can resolve conflicts or permit an exception, if one
is allowed. An exception records the affected scope, rationale, owning role,
compensating evidence, and expiry or reconsideration trigger; link its ARD when
consequential. Do not silently weaken an inherited invariant or waive a
constraint that the owning authority declares non-waivable.
